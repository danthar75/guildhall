Guildhall: #exchange, in the game

An addon that brings our guild exchange into WoW, so members can see who's buying and selling without alt-tabbing to Discord, and more of our gold stays in the guild.

Nothing is built yet. Before any work starts, I'd like to know whether leadership wants this.

Why

Our #exchange channel gives members a place to buy from and sell to each other before going to the auction house. The pinned format is simple:

LF [item] I [will / will not] provide materials I am [open / not open] to CoD

But people are in the game when they're trading, and Discord is in another window. Posts get buried, nobody can tell whether a seller is online, and old requests stay up after they've been filled. Guildhall puts the same board where the trading actually happens.

What it does

Post in our format

Buying (LF) or selling, the item (shift-click it to link it), materials yes/no, CoD yes/no, and an optional note for price, quantity or timing.

Shared across the guild

Every member running Guildhall sees the same board. Listings stay up when the poster logs off.

Clears itself

Listings expire after 7 days. The poster can renew one, edit it, or take it down early.

Shows who's online

A green dot means the poster is online, on any of their characters. Grey means offline.

One click to respond

If they're online, Whisper opens chat with a message ready to send. If they're offline, Mail fills in a letter at the mailbox.

Keeps Discord in the loop

Copy for Discord turns any listing into a post in the pinned format, so members without the addon still see it in #exchange.

The Whisper button opens chat like this. You can change the text, and you press Enter yourself:

To Thalwyn: Hi! Saw your Guildhall listing for [Robe of the Archmage]. Are you still looking?

Copy for Discord produces this:

LF Robe of the Archmage I will provide materials I am open to CoD Note: have the cloth, need a tailor. Tipping 20g. (Thalwyn, in game via Guildhall)

Ground rules

1

It never sends anything for you

Whispers and mail are only filled in. You read them and press Send. Gold, items and CoD are always attached by hand.

2

It stays within Forever's addon rules

Forever uses the same addon restrictions as Midnight. Guildhall doesn't touch combat data. Inside dungeons and raids, Blizzard limits addon messages and guild data, so the board shares new listings in town and the open world, and catches up when you leave an instance.

3

Your alts are shared only if you agree

Guildhall groups your characters so the online dot and "my listings" work across alts. The first time, it shows you which characters it would link, and you can leave any of them out.

4

Officers can clean up

Officers can remove any listing. Who counts as an officer is set by a short tag in the Guild Information text, for example [Guildhall officers=2], so leadership controls it with no addon settings to hand out.

5

There's a paper trail

Each member's addon keeps an activity log of every listing created, edited, renewed or removed, and who passed it along. If something odd happens, officers can compare logs.

What it won't do (for now)

  • No direct Discord connection. WoW addons can't access the internet, so there's no bot. Copy for Discord is the bridge.
  • No price checking or auction house scanning.
  • Members without the addon won't see the in-game board. They still have #exchange.
  • No Emergence art yet. The look can take on guild colours, a logo or a banner later without reworking the addon.

Built to grow

The Exchange is the first feature on a Guildhall core that's meant to hold more. Settings, guild messaging, the activity log and the window are shared, so later tools can be added without starting over. If leadership has ideas for what the guild needs next, I'd like to hear them.
